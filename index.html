How I work

The creative is the targeting.

Meta and TikTok read what an ad shows and says to decide who sees it. So I run media buying and creative as one system, and every result feeds the next round.

Strategy

One audience, five states of mind.

People don't buy the first time they see an ad. They move from not knowing they have a problem to choosing a brand. I brief creative for each stage of awareness, so every ad has one job, and I match each stage to where it runs and how it's judged.

Cold: broad prospectingWarm: retargeting and past buyers

Creative diversity in the Andromeda era.

Meta's Andromeda system decides who sees an ad largely from the ad itself, so the creative now does the targeting. It also groups ads it sees as alike: the industry calls this an Entity ID, though it isn't an official Meta term. Ten near-identical versions of one ad compete for the same slot instead of reaching ten different groups of people.

So I don't scale by making more variations. I scale by making ads that are genuinely different, so each one finds its own buyers, then I iterate on the winners.

  • Broad, consolidated campaigns. Few campaigns and broad targeting give the system room to match each concept to its buyer. On one premium accessories account, broad beat luxury-brand interests: 9.25x ROAS.
  • Fatigue and similarity are different problems. Fatigue (rising frequency and CPA on a winner) is fixed by rotating in fresh executions. Similarity (new ads that never get delivery) is fixed by a new concept, not a new headline.
  • Read the diversity signal. I check Meta's creative diversity rating in Ads Manager alongside hook rate and CPA, and brief the next batch to fill the gaps.
